Permits & Approvals

Laurens County
Permit Process

Laurens County Building & Zoning is located at 100 Hillcrest Square, right in downtown Laurens. For Laurens city addresses, walking applications in is the simplest approach and we do it regularly. All porch permits are required regardless of size. Fees run $100–$350 and approval takes 7–14 business days. Structural repairs above a certain valuation threshold may also require a permit — we assess this during the estimate visit and advise you accordingly. We handle all paperwork from start to finish.

Burch Contracting handles all permit filings in Laurens County on your behalf at no extra charge. We schedule and attend the final inspection, and deliver the certificate of occupancy before the project closes.
