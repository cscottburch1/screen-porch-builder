Yes. Screened porches are permitted structures in both Greenville County and Laurens County. Permits cover structural framing, roof attachment, and any electrical. Approval typically takes 7–14 business days. We file all permits on your behalf — it's included in our service, not an extra charge.

A screened porch uses fixed or removable fiberglass or aluminum screen panels — open to the breeze year-round.
