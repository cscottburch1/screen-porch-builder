Permit Process

Most Joanna-area properties fall under Laurens County Building Codes. Permit fees for a residential screened porch in Laurens County typically run $150–400 depending on project valuation, and approval takes 7–14 business days. Rural properties outside any incorporated town limits go through Laurens County directly. We verify the jurisdiction for every project before filing and handle the complete permit package — application, follow-up, and all required inspections through final sign-off — at no extra charge.

Laurens County is our home territory. We've been working here since 1995 and know the building department staff and process well. For straightforward residential porch projects, we rarely see delays beyond the standard 7–14 day window.
