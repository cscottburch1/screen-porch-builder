Common questions about screened porches in Simpsonville, Fountain Inn, Gray Court, and Laurens, SC.

How much does a screened porch cost in Simpsonville, SC in 2026?+
In 2026, screened porches in Simpsonville range from around $8,000–$12,000 for a basic aluminum-framed patio enclosure to $25,000–$40,000+ for a full custom wood-framed porch with gable roof, ceiling fans, and EZ Breeze windows. The Greenville County average in 2025 was $28,400 for a 240 sq ft project. Use our Cost Calculator above, or call for a free written on-site estimate.

Yes — Farm at Wells Creek (Yellowstone & Tantalus Trails off I-385, Gray Court) is one of our most active neighborhoods. New Meritage Homes come with back patios that are perfect for screened porch additions. We know the HOA guidelines, the common floor plans, and how to match your home's exterior exactly. We're based 5 minutes away in Gray Court.
Do screened porches require a permit in Greenville or Laurens County, SC?+
Yes. Screened porches are permitted structures in both Greenville County and Laurens County. Permits cover structural framing, roof attachment, and any electrical. Approval typically takes 7–14 business days. We file all permits on your behalf — it's included in our service, not an extra charge.
What screen type is best for Gray Court properties near ponds or woods?+
For properties near water or woods in Gray Court, Laurens County, and rural Upstate SC, we strongly recommend No-See-Um screen (20×20 mesh). Standard fiberglass (18×14 mesh) lets no-see-um gnats and biting midges pass right through — a huge problem in this area. The no-see-um upgrade typically adds only $150–$300 to a standard project and makes a dramatic difference in comfort.
How long does it take to build a screened porch in Fountain Inn or Gray Court?+
Once your permit is approved (7–14 business days), most screened porch builds complete in 3–7 business days. Patio enclosures are often 2–3 days. Repairs and re-screening are usually same-week or next-week. We give you a confirmed start date before we leave your property — no indefinite waiting.
Can you screen in my existing concrete patio without building from scratch?+
Absolutely — patio enclosures are one of our most popular services and a great way to save money. We attach a post-and-beam frame to your existing slab and add screening panels around the perimeter. This is ideal for new subdivision homes where the builder left a back patio slab. No new concrete needed, lower cost, and still a beautiful finished result.
What is the difference between a screened porch and an EZ Breeze porch?+
A screened porch uses fixed or removable fiberglass or aluminum screen panels — open to the breeze year-round. An EZ Breeze porch uses vinyl-glazed panels that slide or fold open, giving you a true 3-season or 4-season room. EZ Breeze panels keep out cold, rain, and pollen while maintaining airflow when open. We offer both — we'll recommend the best fit based on your goals and budget.
