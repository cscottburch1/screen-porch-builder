Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Burch Contracting ("we," "our," or "us"), operating ScreenPorchBuilder.com, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and who we share it with.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you submit an estimate request or contact form:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City, ZIP code, and subdivision (optional)
  • Project type and description (optional)

We do not collect payment information, Social Security numbers, or sensitive personal data through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your estimate request or inquiry
  • Schedule a free on-site consultation if requested
  • Follow up on an open estimate (one follow-up only if you don't respond)

We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional materials. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

Our contact forms use Web3Forms (web3forms.com) to process and deliver your submission to us. Web3Forms receives the information you submit through our forms. We recommend reviewing Web3Forms' Privacy Policy for details on their data handling.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). Google may collect your IP address when your browser requests these fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking or advertising scripts.

4. Cookies

ScreenPorchBuilder.com does not set its own cookies. Web3Forms and Google Fonts may set their own cookies as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

Estimate requests and contact form submissions are retained in our email inbox. We delete completed or outdated inquiries within 12 months. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of any follow-up communication from us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
