Permits & Approvals

Fountain Inn's Dual-County
Permit Situation

Fountain Inn's city limits straddle the Greenville–Laurens county line, which means which permit office handles your porch depends entirely on your specific property's location. Addresses within city limits may file through the City of Fountain Inn's own building department. Addresses in unincorporated areas outside city limits file with either Greenville County Building Codes (301 University Ridge, Greenville) or Laurens County Building & Zoning (100 Hillcrest Square, Laurens) depending on which county your parcel falls in. Fees run $150–500 and approval takes 7–14 business days in either county. We identify the correct jurisdiction using county parcel data before filing anything — this is not something you should have to figure out on your own.

Burch Contracting has filed permits on both sides of Fountain Inn's county line dozens of times. We know both offices, both processes, and how to keep your project moving regardless of which county has jurisdiction.
